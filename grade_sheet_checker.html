Grade Sheet Checker

Drop one, a few, or hundreds of semester grade-sheet PDFs. Every subject row and every total is recomputed from the marks and compared with what is printed, then you can download the whole report as Excel.

What is checked

  • Grade matches the marks (grade scale)
  • Grade Points match the grade for those marks
  • Credit Points = Credits × Grade Points
  • Credits per course = T + P + PR; total T-P-PR and Total Credits
  • Total Marks = sum of subject marks
  • Total Credit Points = sum of the Credit Points column
  • Maximum Marks = number of subjects × 100
  • Percentage = total marks ÷ maximum marks × 100
  • Grand Total Credit Points = Total Credits × 10
  • SGPA = Σ(Credits × Grade Points) ÷ Total Credits

Calculations use exact fractions and ROUND_HALF_UP to the number of decimals printed on the sheet — no floating-point drift.
